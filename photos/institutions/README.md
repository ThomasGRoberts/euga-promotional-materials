# Featured-site photograph library

The institution list in `data/institutions.json` mirrors the published spreadsheet's final **Institutions** tab (gid `1659852286`). Each row retains the spreadsheet's exact institution, city, and country text, with a local photograph path added for the carousel.

New photographs in this directory have creator, license, source page, and original size recorded in `sources.json`. Seven already-downloaded institution photographs remain in their city folders; their attribution records are in `photos/sources.json` and the matching city `sources.json` files.

The carousel shows only two featured-site slides per cycle. It selects from rows with locally available photographs, then rotates to another pair at the next cycle. **Blue Sky Technology Company, Bucharest** is listed in the sheet, but a trustworthy match for that company's website or photograph has not yet been established. Its row remains in the catalog, but is omitted from slide rotation until an authentic image can be supplied. Do not substitute another company or a generic Bucharest photograph for this entry.

The presentation no longer includes featured-site slides. The Palais des Nations is not a separate row in the spreadsheet's institutions tab. UNIDIR's official contact page lists the Palais des Nations as its address, so the UNIDIR catalog image depicts that site.

# Banner photo library

This library contains high-resolution city and institutional imagery for the click-to-advance banner presentation. Images were selected from Wikimedia Commons and retain creator, license, source-page, and original-dimension information in each folder's `sources.json` and in the combined `photos/sources.json`.

## Coverage

- `metz`: city views, Georgia Tech-Europe, Robert Schuman House
- `strasbourg`: city views, European Parliament
- `trier`: city views
- `luxembourg-city`: city views, EU institutional district and Court of Justice context
- `brussels`: city views, European Parliament, European Commission, NATO Headquarters
- `amsterdam`: city views
- `the-hague`: city views, Peace Palace, International Criminal Court
- `bucharest`: city views, NATO Force Integration Unit
- `berlin`: city views, Bundestag
- `munich`: city views
- `nuremberg`: city views, Palace of Justice and Nuremberg Trials courtroom
- `geneva`: city views, United Nations Geneva, International Telecommunication Union
- `bern`: city views
- `paris`: city views, Assemblée Nationale, French Senate, Quai d'Orsay, European Space Agency
- `vienna`: city views, Vienna International Centre, IAEA, United Nations Office at Vienna

`review.html` is a browser-based contact sheet for reviewing the complete library. The photo set can be regenerated or refined with `scripts/download_banner_photos.py`.
